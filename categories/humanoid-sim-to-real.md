# System identification and sim-to-real

Track: Humanoid Robotics · Level: advanced · ID: LL-051

## Build challenge

Fit a simulated actuator parameter on training traces and report prediction error on withheld traces; document the remaining hardware gap.

## Prerequisites

Python, vector geometry, and elementary linear algebra. Simulation and control lessons also require mechanics and feedback-control foundations.

## Work plan

1. Define inputs, units, versions, constraints, and a measurable outcome.
2. Read the relevant source material and reproduce its smallest applicable example.
3. Build the challenge incrementally. Keep the baseline and candidate separate.
4. Test a normal case, a boundary case, and a failure case. Record all three.
5. Save the result, dependencies, limitations, and enough evidence for another learner to reproduce it.

## Evidence to submit

- Code or design files and exact commands.
- A small input dataset with provenance and applicable permissions.
- Baseline, observed result, errors or failures, and a short explanation.
- Version and dependency records; no secrets or private participant information.
